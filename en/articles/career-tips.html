10 Simple Career Tips

In this article I want to talk about 10 simple tips that will help improve your life overall and become more calm. If a person is full of strength and energy, then he will certainly succeed, so it is necessary to take care of both his condition and his image. This is what the following tips say:

1. They promised to call back - call back

Tell me, how often have people (companies) promised to call you back and did not? Personally, I encounter this kind of attitude all the time. Even if you have no news or nothing to report, but you promised to call back, call back. People will appreciate it.

I always note when companies promise to call back in 2 hours and they call back. I try not to deal with the rest.

2. If you are late, warn

Everyone experiences annoying misunderstandings on the eve of meetings: traffic jams, elevators, urgent matters... And no one is immune from troubles, but still, if there is a chance that you will be late, report it.

I have repeatedly witnessed how a large number of meeting participants were waiting for the arrival of 1-2 directors, who did not even bother to warn about being late - this is perceived extremely negatively and nervously.

3. Rest

Follow a work-rest schedule, get a good night's sleep, and then you will have more strength and energy to solve problems during the day. By constantly trying to cut corners, you will most likely not gain much time, but you will spend a lot of effort.

Nowadays, this rule is especially relevant, because Without time to rest, we weaken our nervous system and undermine our health. Your family and friends also suffer from the endless race.

4. Don’t laugh at other people’s mistakes and mistakes

Everyone has difficult days, tasks and projects. Do not rush to blame and ridicule other people's mistakes and failures. People will learn, improve, but relationships will be damaged forever. As a leader, you must create an environment in your organization that is tolerant of mistakes.

In addition, your tolerance for other people's mistakes will turn into your benefit when you make a mistake yourself.

5. Be polite

The city takes politeness. Unfortunately, politeness is a rarity these days; everyone around us has become callous, cold and fixated on some kind of ambition, disdainful of others.

Politeness is not weakness, politeness is not flirting, politeness is an adequate model of behavior. Be polite please!

6. Know how to admit your mistakes and apologize

I was wrong, and most likely I will make many mistakes in the future. The main thing is to have courage and admit your mistakes, don’t get hung up on them. A mistake is just an attempt to take a step in an unknown new direction, so who knows what you will stumble upon tomorrow.

If you made a mistake and someone noticed it, don’t lean on it, but simply apologize - this will nip the emerging conflict in the bud, and show your opponent that you are a strong person.

By the way, many famous discoveries were made by mistake, for example, the well-known yellow stickers.

7. Don't complain

Firstly, it is meaningless and does not particularly affect anything, secondly, accept the fact: things and events are in the position in which they should be or in which you allowed them to be.

We need to get together and take action.

8. Try to tell the truth

I understand that it is very difficult, but in the long run, telling the truth is beneficial. You don't have to remember who, what and when you said. You will also be able to sleep peacefully at night and not be afraid that at the most crucial moment in your career, a little lie will block the way to the top.

Despite the fact that I prefer when they tell me the truth directly and to my face, I still advise you not to throw it like a wet rag in your face, it should be served carefully, wrapped as if in a warm blanket. Respect your interlocutor.

9. Do what needs to be done first

Obviously, but many try to do what they want to do, and not what they need to do. While you are full of strength and energy, try to do the most important, complex and tedious work first, then pleasant chores will become a kind of relaxation and reward for you.

By the way, if you need to move a lot of boxes or crates, then start with the heaviest ones, the same principle works with news.

10. When quarreling, do not drive your opponent into a corner and do not use dirty tricks

Respect your opponent and take care of your opponent. Your rivals make you stronger. Our world is so dynamic and changeable that rivals and friends often change places. By driving your opponent into a corner, you deprive him of the opportunity to save face and get out of the situation with dignity.

If you give your opponent the opportunity to retreat, then he will not resist you so fiercely. In addition, it is quite possible that you will have to work together on other projects in the future.
