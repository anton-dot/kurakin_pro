About the product

A layout can fit on paper and still feel wrong

Will the sofa leave enough space to walk past? Can the wardrobe sit next to the door? Where will the outlets end up? It is easier to check these things before renovating.

With RoomVibe, you can draw rooms, arrange furniture, try finishes, and walk through your home in 3D. Don't like a layout? Change it and take another look.

Core features

From floor plan to 3D walkthrough

01

Draw your own floor plan

Create custom-shaped rooms, adjust walls and dimensions, and place doors and windows. Plan an apartment or a multi-floor home.

02

Walk through your future home

Switch from the plan to a 3D view and explore the rooms in first person. Check walking space, furniture placement, and how doors open.

03

Try furniture at the right size

Choose from over 40 types of furniture, appliances, bathroom fixtures, and decor. Adjust positions and dimensions, including sofa beds.

04

Try finishes before committing

Change wall colours, floor coverings, ceilings, and wallpaper. Use your own images for wall finishes and see how they look with the furniture.

05

Plan outlets and utilities

Place outlets, switches, lighting, electrical routes, water pipes, and heating. Use individual wall views to plan their position and height.

06

Keep your favourite versions

Copy projects, undo changes, and export floor plans and 3D views as PNG. Export project files for backup or transfer between devices.

Workflow

Plan, inspect, and compare options

Build a layout

  • Start with an empty plan or an editable example.
  • Define rooms, openings, and floors.
  • Place furniture, appliances, and structures.

Work through the details

  • Inspect the interior in 3D and walk through it.
  • Choose finishes, photos, and materials.
  • Add utility fixtures and routing.

Keep the result

  • Compare ideas using project copies.
  • Export plan or 3D views as PNG images.
  • Export JSON for backup or transfer between devices.

Try it before you renovate

Start your own floor plan or explore an example. It is easier to move furniture and walls on screen than after the renovation. The app interface is currently in Russian.
