RoomVibe

Floor plans, furniture, finishes, and building utilities in one editable apartment model.

Open RoomVibe ↗ Public web build on GitHub ↗

About the product

One model instead of disconnected drawings

A floor plan, furniture layout, and utility drawings often live in different files. Move one wall and some of those documents are already out of date.

RoomVibe brings planning and interior layout together. You can adjust room geometry, place objects, inspect the result in 3D, and save alternative project versions. The application interface is currently in Russian.

Core features

From wall dimensions to a walkthrough

01

2D floor planning

Create rectangular, L-shaped, or custom-shaped rooms. Edit wall geometry, dimensions, ceiling height, thickness, doors, and windows.

02

3D views and walkthroughs

Navigate a perspective view or walk through the space in first person. Open doors, switch lights, and change between day and night.

03

Furniture and finishes

Place furniture, appliances, fixtures, and décor. Adjust their size, position, and colours, as well as floor, wall, and ceiling finishes.

04

Utility layers

Map electrical, water, and heating systems with fixtures and routes. Work with an electrical panel and junction boxes in dedicated editors.

05

Individual wall views

See an interior wall in 2D, including doors, windows, outlets, switches, and utility routes at their installation heights.

06

Local projects and export

Save automatically in the browser, undo changes, import or export JSON projects, and export plan and 3D views to PNG.

Workflow

Plan, inspect, and compare options

Build a layout

  • Start with an empty plan or an editable example.
  • Define rooms, openings, and floors.
  • Place furniture, appliances, and structures.

Work through the details

  • Inspect the interior in 3D and walk through it.
  • Choose finishes, photos, and materials.
  • Add utility fixtures and routing.

Keep the result

  • Compare ideas using project copies.
  • Export plan or 3D views as PNG images.
  • Export JSON for backup or transfer between devices.

Under the hood

Practical boundaries

Built with Flutter

RoomVibe runs in the browser. Its 3D scene uses programmatically rendered models, not photorealistic visualization.

Locally stored data

Projects and photos are saved in the browser. Moving them to another device requires JSON export and import; cloud sync is not part of the current version.

Planning, not engineering sign-off

RoomVibe is for layouts and early utility sketches. It does not replace load calculations, electrical protection design, or certified construction documents.
