About the product

From CV creation to interview outcomes

CV Boost supports the personal job-search workflow: create multiple CVs, tailor them to roles, prepare cover letters, and track interviews. Each company record can include the role, stages, meetings, questions, impressions, and final outcome.

CVs, letters, and interview history are stored locally in the browser. The application requires no registration or dedicated server, and the installed PWA can be used offline after its first launch.

Core features

From content to the next stage

01

Russian and English versions

Language versions are edited independently, while empty sections can be copied from an existing translation.

02

Six templates

PRO, PRO 2, PRO 3, CLASSIC, MINIMAL, and ATS range from expressive layouts to a strict single-column structure.

03

CV checks

Local validation identifies missing content and provides guidance for applicant tracking systems.

04

PDF and Word

The finished CV exports to PDF or DOCX using the selected template and compact, standard, or relaxed density.

05

Cover letters

Letters are created and stored independently from CVs, with a ready-made sample and one-click clipboard copy.

06

Interview tracker

Company, role, stage, status, meetings, questions, and impressions stay together with the submitted CV version.

07

Portable backups

CVs can be duplicated, exported to a portable backup file, and restored through the import flow.

08

Local and registration-free

Data stays in the browser, while the installed PWA continues to work offline after its first launch.

Workflow

Manage a job search without extra services

Content

  • experience, projects, and case studies;
  • education and courses;
  • skills, languages, and certificates;
  • publications, awards, and volunteering.

Interviews

  • company, role, stage, and status;
  • multiple meetings with one company;
  • questions, impressions, and outcomes;
  • link to the submitted CV version.

Output

  • live preview;
  • page-break protection for content blocks;
  • general and ATS checks;
  • PDF, Word, and backup export.

Architecture

A local tool for personal data

Local-first

CVs, photos, letters, and interview history are stored in the browser's local database and do not require a user account.

Client-side export

PDF, Word, and backup files are generated directly in the application without sending CV content to a dedicated server.

Progressive web application

CV Boost can be installed as a PWA and continues to work offline after the initial launch.

Responsive interface

The editor, preview, and media library are designed for desktop, tablet, and mobile screens.
