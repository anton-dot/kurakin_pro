Why this exists

Technical debt becomes easier to prioritize when it has a cost

Technical debt is rarely just a code-quality problem. It consumes engineering time, slows changes, increases operational work, and competes with product delivery.

This calculator deliberately keeps the model simple. The goal is not to manufacture precision, but to create a defensible first estimate that can start a better prioritization conversation.
