One project

A book rarely lives in one document.

The manuscript is in one place. Characters are somewhere else. Plot ideas live in notes. Earlier versions are scattered across folders. Publishing often requires another tool.

B-Maker keeps the work around the book together, while letting you start with nothing more than a blank chapter.

Revision

Rewrite without destroying yesterday's work.

Keep several revisions of a chapter or scene inside the project. Compare them, choose the current version, and return to an earlier idea when you need it.

No more folders full of chapter-final.docx, chapter-final-2.docx, and chapter-REAL-final.docx.

Local-first

One book. One file. Yours.

A B-Maker project is a portable .bmaker file. Your manuscript does not have to live in somebody else's cloud.

Keep it on your computer, put it into the backup system you already use, or move it through the cloud drive you already trust.

No mandatory cloud storage
No server dependency for your manuscript
