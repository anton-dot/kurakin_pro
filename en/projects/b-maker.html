From articles to a book

Start with an article. Grow into a book.

You do not have to begin with a 500-page novel. Write one article. Then another. Find your rhythm, collect ideas, and build up material without committing to a huge structure on day one.

Keep active pieces in Drafts and move finished work to Published. Collapse that folder and completed articles stop cluttering the working view, while remaining searchable and available inside the same project.

Over time, a series of articles can become the raw material for a larger work — and when it does, the notes, drafts, versions, and context are already there.

Start writing first. Let the bigger structure appear when you need it.

One project

Long-form work rarely lives in one document.

The text is in one place. Notes are somewhere else. Ideas live in another app. Earlier versions are scattered across folders. For books, characters and plot information add even more pieces.

B-Maker keeps the work around your writing together, while letting you start with nothing more than a blank page.

Characters change. Relationships evolve. Plotlines cross. Events affect what happens later.

Keep characters, relationships, arcs, locations, and plotlines next to the manuscript and connect them to the places where they matter.

Revision

Rewrite without destroying yesterday's work.

Keep several revisions of a chapter, scene, or article inside the project. Compare them, choose the current version, and return to an earlier idea when you need it.

No more folders full of final.docx, final-2.docx, and REAL-final.docx.

Local-first

One project. One file. Yours.

A B-Maker project is a portable .bmaker file. Your books, articles, and notes do not have to live in somebody else's cloud.

Keep it on your computer, put it into the backup system you already use, or move it through the cloud drive you already trust.
