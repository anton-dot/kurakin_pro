Ready-to-share summary

Model: annual spend = paid seats × monthly seat cost × 12 + overlapping SaaS spend. Potential savings = unused seats + removable share of underused seats + downgrade savings for active over-tier seats + overlapping tools. Seat categories are capped so they cannot exceed the total paid licenses.

Why it matters

SaaS waste rarely looks like one large expense

It is usually dozens of small leaks: former employee accounts, licenses kept just in case, premium tiers for one feature, and several tools solving nearly the same problem.

The calculator is not a replacement for SaaS management or procurement review. It turns scattered usage issues into a financial number that can be discussed with product owners, IT, Finance, and Procurement.
