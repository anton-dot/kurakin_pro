    Ready-to-share summary

    The bus factor here is intentionally a simple proxy: the minimum number of people able to cover any listed critical area independently. Real organizational risk also depends on knowledge overlap, documentation quality, and how quickly context can be transferred.

    How to use it

    Do not count people. Find fragile knowledge.

    Bus factor is useful less as a team KPI and more as a way to find areas where one vacation, resignation, or overloaded person becomes an operational risk.

    The first practical move is usually simple: give a second person enough ownership to complete the full loop independently — change, release, diagnose, and recover the critical component.
