Ready-to-share summary

This calculator uses a simple event-based model: the allowed bad-event share equals 100% − SLO. The downtime budget is the equivalent share of time. A real SLO still depends on choosing the right SLI and measuring the user outcome that actually matters.

How to read it

Burn rate matters more than pretty nines

An SLO alone does not tell the team whether reliability work is urgent. That becomes visible when the error budget is being consumed faster than time is passing.

A 1× burn rate means the budget is being spent roughly in line with the window. Above 1× is an early signal that, if quality stays the same, the budget will run out before the window ends.
