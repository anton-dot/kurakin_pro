B-Maker vs Plottr

Plottr specializes in visual outlining and story bibles. B-Maker is broader but currently less specialized: it keeps planning beside the manuscript, revisions, checks, illustrations, and publishing output.

Short answer

Choose Plottr if visual plotting, templates, timelines, series planning, and a mature story bible are the center of your workflow. Try B-Maker if you want to plan and then keep writing the actual manuscript inside the same portable project.

Plottr is a specialist

Plottr is built around visual planning. Its timeline, scene cards, plotlines, story bibles, characters, places, tags, templates, filters, and series views go much deeper than B-Maker's current planning layer. For writers who want to architect a novel before drafting, that specialization is a real advantage.

Plottr also has a Pro offering with web access, cloud storage, automatic synchronization, offline support and live collaboration. B-Maker does not currently offer an equivalent backend service.

B-Maker tries to remove the handoff

The main B-Maker idea is that planning should not necessarily end with an export into another writing program. A plotline, character, location or story note sits beside the manuscript, and the writer can move directly from planning into a chapter and continue revising and publishing in the same project.

That makes B-Maker broader, but it also means Plottr is currently stronger in areas where specialization matters: visual timeline depth, templates, series-level planning and collaboration.

Local-first versus connected cloud workflow

B-Maker is local-first. A book is a portable .bmaker project and there is no mandatory B-Maker cloud. Plottr supports desktop use and, with Plottr Pro, a connected workflow across web, desktop and mobile with cloud synchronization.

Neither model is universally better. Some writers prioritize control and a simple portable file; others value automatic synchronization and collaboration more.

When Plottr makes more sense

  • Visual timelines are central to how you build a story.
  • You want established plotting templates.
  • You are planning a multi-book series.
  • You want cloud sync and collaboration through Plottr Pro.
  • You are happy to move from planning into Word, Scrivener or another writing tool.

When B-Maker may fit better

  • You want the planning layer and actual manuscript in the same project.
  • You want revisions, text checks, illustrations and publishing output after drafting.
  • You prefer local-first project ownership.
  • You want a simpler workflow and do not need a large template library.

Verdict

Plottr is currently the stronger dedicated plotting product. B-Maker's advantage is not deeper plotting; it is continuity. The same project can move from idea to manuscript to revision to export without a planned handoff into another writing application.
