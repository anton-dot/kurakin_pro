Idea MapVisual planning

Writing software with an idea map connected to the manuscript.

A visual canvas is more useful when it is not a separate universe. In B-Maker, notes, chapters, scenes, characters and plotlines can live on the map as part of the same writing project.

The key difference

B-Maker's Idea Map is not just an embedded drawing board. It can work with real project entities, navigate back to the manuscript, and turn visual notes or groups into actual chapters and sections.

Ideas do not arrive in chapter order

Writers often think spatially before they think hierarchically. A character, conflict, location, question and possible ending may appear on the same day with no obvious place in the outline. A freeform board is a natural place to keep those thoughts while the structure is still forming.

One project, not two applications

Traditional workflows often split thinking and writing: mind map in one tool, manuscript in another. That creates copying, stale information and the question of which representation is current. B-Maker keeps both views inside the same project.

Use real writing entities

The map can include chapters, sections, characters, plotlines, images, planning cards and ordinary notes. You can add an entire manuscript branch, jump from a node back to the editor or planning screen, and keep visual relationships close to the source material.

Turn ideas into manuscript

Notes and groups can become real chapters or sections with an initial text version. This is the important transition: the map is allowed to feed the manuscript instead of becoming a visual archive you stop updating after drafting begins.

Still freeform when you need it

The Idea Map supports multiple boards, links, groups, stacks, magnetic areas, search, filters, minimap and automatic layout. It can therefore remain exploratory without forcing every thought into the book hierarchy immediately.

When a dedicated mind-mapping tool may be better

If the canvas itself is the final product, you need advanced diagramming, or you want a completely independent visual thinking environment, a specialist tool such as Scapple or a general whiteboard may fit better. B-Maker's advantage is integration with the writing project, not maximum diagramming depth.
