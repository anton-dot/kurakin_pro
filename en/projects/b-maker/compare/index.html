Choose by the problem you are solving: replace a heavier writing tool, keep articles and a future book together, work local-first, plan visually, or use the same writing system across several operating systems.

What B-Maker is trying to do differently

B-Maker treats long-form writing as one portable project: books or article collections, manuscript, versions, collections, idea boards, characters and plotlines, notes, analytics, illustrations, and publishing preparation stay together. It is local-first by design and exposes the simple writing path before the deeper tools.
