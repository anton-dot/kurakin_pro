B-Maker vs Scapple

Scapple is a mature freeform canvas for getting ideas out of your head. B-Maker's planned Idea Map has a different goal: make visual thinking part of the book project itself.

Important: B-Maker Idea Map is still coming

If you need a polished freeform mapping tool today, Scapple is the safer choice. This comparison explains the design difference B-Maker is building toward rather than pretending an unreleased feature is already equivalent.

Scapple's strength is freedom

Literature & Latte describes Scapple as a virtual sheet of paper: double-click anywhere to make a note, then connect notes with lines or arrows. It deliberately avoids forcing a hierarchy. That makes it excellent for brainstorming where you do not yet know what the structure should be.

Scapple also supports grouping, note appearance customization, stacking notes, export and print, and dragging notes into Scrivener. Its simplicity is part of the design rather than a missing feature.

B-Maker is aiming for a different kind of map

The planned B-Maker Idea Map is not intended to become a general-purpose whiteboard. The useful distinction is that a node can eventually represent something the rest of the book already understands: a character, a scene, an event, a location, a chapter or simply an idea.

That opens the door for the same underlying book information to appear in different views later — for example, a visual map, a story grid, a chapter outline or a timeline — without manually rebuilding the same story in separate tools.

When Scapple makes more sense

  • You want freeform visual brainstorming today.
  • You do not want the tool to impose meaning on connections.
  • You already use Scrivener and like the Scapple → Scrivener workflow.
  • You need a mature native Mac application.

When B-Maker's approach may fit better

  • You want ideas to remain attached to the book project instead of a separate board.
  • You want characters, scenes and chapters to be real project entities rather than just labels on boxes.
  • You want to move between planning and manuscript writing without changing applications.
  • You prefer one portable project file.

Verdict

Scapple and B-Maker are not trying to solve exactly the same problem. Scapple is stronger when the map itself should stay freeform. B-Maker's opportunity is the opposite: turn visual thinking into another view of the same book. Until Idea Map ships, Scapple remains the more complete mapping product.
