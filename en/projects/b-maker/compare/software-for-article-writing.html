Article writingRecurring publishing

Article software should help before, during and after the draft.

The useful workflow includes the brief, sources, structure, revisions, publication stage and the archive that remains useful after the article is published.

The difference

A text editor helps you write one article. A writing project helps you keep the article useful before and after publication.

Start with a brief, not a blank template

Before drafting, it can help to capture a working title, main thesis, reader outcome, target audience, tone, publication channel, editorial requirements, source material and the questions each section should answer.

B-Maker's Article Wizard creates a planning scaffold around those decisions. The scaffold is guidance, not generated prose that must be accepted.

Keep drafts and published work in the same project

An article stops being active when it is published, but it does not stop being useful. Published pieces can remain searchable while organizational folders keep them out of book export and out of the current working view.

Use versions when the article changes direction

A major edit, a different opening, an editor's requested rewrite or a shorter publication version can deserve its own named version. Compare rather than overwrite, especially when several outlets or audiences need different cuts.

Plan publication separately from structure

The article structure answers what the reader will see. The publication plan answers when research, draft, editorial revision, proofreading and publication should happen. Deadlines and checklists should not become fake headings inside the article.

Use AI as an optional assistant

AI can help inspect clarity, generate revision candidates, analyze audience hypotheses or propose structure. The useful boundary is the same as for books: explicit scope, reviewable output and no silent replacement of the current text.

Let articles grow into larger work

A series of related articles can later become research material for a book. Keeping drafts, published pieces, notes, versions and thematic collections in one project makes that transition much cheaper.

What to look for in article writing software

Look for briefs, reusable research, versions, a published archive, publication stages, flexible export, collections and a workflow that still works when ten separate articles become one larger project.
