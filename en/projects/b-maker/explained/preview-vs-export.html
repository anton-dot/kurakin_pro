PublishingPreview vs output

If you can export the book, why preview it first?

Because “does the book look right?” and “give me the final file” are different steps.

The distinction

Preview is inspection. Export is production.

Writing structure is not page structure

A section may need to exist in the project while its heading should be hidden in the book. A working branch may stay in the project while being excluded from output. Page breaks, cover, illustrations, and current versions only become meaningful when the whole book is assembled.

Preview creates a cheap failure

It is better to discover an accidental blank page, wrong heading, stale version, broken illustration flow, or awkward page break before generating and sending the deliverable.

Export has a destination

DOCX may be right for an editor, EPUB for an ebook workflow, PDF for a controlled reading copy, Markdown or TXT for another tool. Export answers “what artifact do I need now?”

Screenshot: preview then exportTwo-stage composition: full-book preview with page boundaries, then export dialog offering DOCX, EPUB, PDF, TXT and Markdown.assets/img/bmaker/bmaker-explained-preview-export-01.webp

The useful rule

Preview whenever presentation matters. Export when you know what you are producing and for whom.
