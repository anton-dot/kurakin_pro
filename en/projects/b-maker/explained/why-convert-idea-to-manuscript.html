Idea MapTransition

Why should a note be able to become a chapter?

Because planning is most useful when it can gradually turn into the real work instead of ending with a copy-paste ceremony.

The principle

Structure should appear when the idea has earned structure, and the transition should preserve the material already created.

Ideas begin cheaper than chapters

A note can be vague, provisional and badly named. That is useful early. A manuscript section carries stronger expectations: position, current text, revision history and eventual output.

Conversion marks a change of maturity

When a note or group becomes stable enough, turning it into a real section says: this is no longer only thinking material; it now belongs to the manuscript.

The important part is continuity

The initial material follows the transition. The writer does not need to recreate the thought in another hierarchy and then remember which planning artifact can be deleted.
