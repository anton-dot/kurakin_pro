B-Maker Explained

AIOptional tool

If you can open ChatGPT separately, why have AI inside B-Maker?

Not because AI should own the writing process. Because the useful place for AI is often inside a specific writing decision.

The principle

The manuscript remains the project. AI is a callable tool that can help inspect, transform, or generate a candidate when you choose to use it.

Context switching has a cost

Copy text into a separate chat, explain what the fragment is, remember which version you sent, copy the result back, and then decide whether the result belongs in the manuscript. For occasional use this is fine. Repeated use creates another parallel workspace.

AI is most useful for bounded tasks

Generate three alternative formulations. Shorten this passage without removing the argument. Find unclear transitions. Suggest questions an editor might ask. Produce candidate blurbs. Summarize a section for a synopsis. These tasks produce proposals, not authority.

Why AI is not mandatory in B-Maker

A writing project must remain fully usable without a model connection. Otherwise the tool becomes dependent on a service for its core purpose. B-Maker keeps AI optional and lets the user configure the OpenAI Responses API rather than making AI the required center of the product.

What AI should not replace

Authorial judgment, factual verification, the decision about voice, and ownership of the current version stay with the author. The model can generate options; the project needs a human decision about what becomes canonical.

The strongest use is reversible

For significant AI rewrites, pair the tool with versions: preserve the original, generate an experiment, compare, then decide.
