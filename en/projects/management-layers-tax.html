Management Layers Tax

How much organizational tax does a normal decision pay while moving through management layers, context handoffs and repeated approvals?

Not scientific. The number of managers is not treated as the problem. This looks only at the cost of routing decisions through the structure.

This is a heuristic, not an organizational efficiency benchmark. Management layers can be useful; the question is whether they become the mandatory route for ordinary work.

What this measures

Not manager count. Route cost.

An extra layer can provide context, accountability and risk control. The problem starts when every decision physically has to travel through it.

At that point the org structure stops being a way to distribute responsibility and starts becoming the workflow.
