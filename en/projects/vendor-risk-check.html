    Ready-to-share summary

    The risk score uses answered questions only and remains preliminary until all 24 are complete. “No / unknown” counts as risk: if a control exists but you cannot verify it, it still needs validation before a decision. This does not replace a security, legal, or procurement review.

    Why it matters

    A vendor problem becomes your problem

    Vendor risk is more than a security questionnaire. A technically mature provider can still create heavy lock-in, unpredictable cost, or a critical operational dependency.

    The tool separates control quality from dependency. That helps identify the next action: request evidence, change contract terms, build a fallback, reduce integration coupling, or choose another vendor.
