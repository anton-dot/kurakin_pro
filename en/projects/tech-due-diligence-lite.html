    Ready-to-share executive summary

    The score uses answered questions only and remains preliminary until all 30 are complete. This is a prioritization tool for initial due diligence, not a substitute for a full technical audit, security review, or legal/compliance assessment.

    What it checks

    More than code and architecture

    Technical health is not defined by a stack alone. The assessment covers architecture, operations, security, delivery, team knowledge, data, and external dependencies.

    The goal is to identify where deeper review is needed next. A low score does not automatically mean a bad company, and a high score does not guarantee the absence of risk. It does help surface the right questions earlier.
