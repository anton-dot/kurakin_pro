Why

The problem may not be how fast people work. It may be how fragmented their attention is.

Meetings are visible on the calendar. Context switches are harder to see: each one looks small, but together they split the day into pieces where complex work becomes difficult.

This calculator is not an argument against every meeting or message. It makes the cost of a permanently reactive operating mode visible.
