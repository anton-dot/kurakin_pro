About the product

Practice beyond memorization

MEnglish takes beginners from their first A0 words and phrases towards confident reading, writing, listening, and speaking. It includes more than 400 flashcards and 22 activity types for children and adults starting English from scratch.

Language does not live in isolation: new words appear in dialogues, visual novels, maths activities, and standalone games. The same material returns in different contexts and gradually becomes active vocabulary.

Core features

One path, many ways to learn

01

A0 English course

Core words, phrases, and grammar form a progressive path designed for complete beginners.

02

400+ cards, 22 activities

Flashcards, matching, image selection, writing, and other formats reinforce material from several angles.

03

Speaking and listening

Audio connects spelling with sound, while speech recognition makes spoken answers part of regular practice.

04

Maths in English

Addition, subtraction, and number bonds develop basic maths while keeping English as the learning language.

05

Visual novels

Kolobok and OZ: Emerald Network turn vocabulary and dialogues into stories with characters and choices.

06

Standalone game modes

Match-3, Galaxy Defense, Word Maze, OZOpoly, and other games practise vocabulary through different rule sets.

Practice formats

Activities adapt to the skill

Beginner topics

  • the alphabet, numbers, and colours;
  • food, animals, and school;
  • body parts, toys, and home;
  • spatial concepts and directions.

Four language skills

  • listen to words, phrases, and dialogues;
  • repeat and check spoken answers;
  • read cards and story dialogue;
  • build and write answers.

Game collection

  • Match-3 and Galaxy Defense;
  • Word Maze and Word Breaker;
  • Memory Match and Word Chain;
  • OZOpoly and Word Battle.

What stands out

More than an exercise collection

Two subjects on one path

Maths activities cover addition, subtraction, and number bonds while keeping English as the language of learning.

Learning inside a story

Visual novels add characters, dialogue, and plot progression so language appears in a meaningful context.

Offline on Android

The mobile version supports continued learning without a permanent internet connection.

A growing story library

New visual novels are added over time, and learners can suggest ideas for future stories.

Try MEnglish

The web version runs directly in the browser, and the Android app is available on Google Play and RuStore.
