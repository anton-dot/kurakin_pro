Busyness Index

Are we actually working? How much of the workday is filled with activity — and how much of that activity turns into finished outcomes?

Not scientific. It does not measure personal productivity. It looks at whether the work environment rewards visible busyness more than progress.

This is a heuristic, not a scientific productivity assessment. It looks at the work environment and rituals, not the value or effectiveness of an individual person.

Why this exists

Busyness is easy to see. Progress is not always

Calendars, Slack, status updates and urgent requests create a very convincing feeling of work. High activity and high outcome velocity are not the same thing.

The goal is not to remove communication. It is to notice when coordination starts consuming more energy than the work itself.
