Ready-to-send summary

Model: Build = engineering during development + opportunity cost + maintenance and infrastructure after launch. Buy = one-time implementation and integration + subscription and internal support. Break-even is a simplified comparison of initial and annual costs.

Why this exists

Build vs Buy is not an argument between engineering and procurement

Internal development often looks cheaper until maintenance, infrastructure, and the opportunity cost of the team are included. Buying looks cheaper until implementation, integrations, and ongoing internal work appear in the model.

This calculator is not designed to make the decision automatically. It makes the financial part visible so the real discussion can move to speed, control, risk, and strategic value.
