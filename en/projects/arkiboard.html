ArkiBoard

Design software systems from the big picture down to individual service modules. Draw diagrams, estimate capacity, and review the design in your browser. No account required.

Open in browser ↗ Download for Windows

One model, multiple diagrams

Draw diagrams separately and the same service can end up as several inconsistent copies. In ArkiBoard, a component has one model record but can appear on multiple diagrams. Each view has its own layout, while the component's responsibilities, technology, and settings stay in sync.

Show the big picture in a discussion, open the relevant service during a review, then follow the requirements, scenarios, and decisions that explain its design.

The editor includes built-in architecture examples. You can explore them or make an editable copy.

From first sketch to architecture review

01

Design on the canvas

Build C4 context, container, and component views with built-in services, modules, databases, caches, queues, and topics. Auto-layout can arrange the whole diagram or just the selection without moving pinned elements. Collapse nested containers without losing external links, or open them for editing.

02

Components and dependencies

Record ownership, criticality, replica counts, or partition settings, depending on the component type. Trace incoming and outgoing dependencies up to three hops or find the shortest path between two nodes. Save a reusable group with its settings and internal links in a personal library.

03

Scenarios and contracts

Show what follows a request or event: which steps run in order, what can happen in parallel, and where compensation is needed. Record timeouts, retries, idempotency, or a dead-letter queue (DLQ) on a connection. Play back the scenario on the diagram or export it as a sequence diagram.

04

Requirements and decisions

Link requirements with metrics, verification criteria, and statuses to specific components and architecture decision records (ADRs). Record alternatives and their trade-offs in an ADR. Compare against a snapshot to see changes in the model without counting moves on the canvas as design changes.

05

Capacity estimates

Compare a baseline workload with tenfold growth. The calculator shows the inputs and formulas behind estimates for traffic, storage, queues, and latency. Use them to discuss scale and assumptions before implementation, not as load-test results.

06

Review and failure analysis

Local rules flag potential single points of failure, cycles, and long chains of synchronous calls. Jump from a finding to the affected components. Failure analysis shows which nodes depend synchronously on a selected component, but does not simulate live failover.

Import, export, and storage

Import existing diagrams

Bring in draw.io projects, PlantUML/C4 or Mermaid diagrams, or Markdown and HTML. ArkiBoard suggests component types and connections. Preview and refine the result before creating the project.

Share review materials

Export a full diagram or a selection as SVG, PNG, or PDF, or all pages as an editable draw.io file. For discussion, share Markdown with requirements, decisions, and Mermaid diagrams, or a standalone HTML report that can play scenarios offline.

Projects without an account

In the web version, work is saved in the current browser. Snapshots let you return to an earlier state but are not a backup: clearing site data removes local projects. Use an .archd file to transfer the full editable model to another device.
