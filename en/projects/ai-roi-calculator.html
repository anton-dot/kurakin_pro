Why this exists

AI can save time and still have weak ROI

A license does not create value by itself. People need to use the tool, AI needs to save more time than it consumes, and the freed capacity needs to become useful output.

That is why the calculator shows not only ROI but also the break-even point in minutes per active user per week. That number is often easier to validate in a pilot than an abstract claim about “productivity improvement.”
