Why this exists

The cost of an incident is rarely the cost of engineers on the call

A large part of incident impact often sits outside engineering: lost revenue, customer support, SLA credits, emergency vendor spend, and follow-up work after service recovery.

This calculator turns reliability from an abstract “we should make the system more resilient” discussion into a financial conversation about the cost of repeating a specific class of failure.
